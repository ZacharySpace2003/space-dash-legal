Space Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Space Dash ("the App," "we," "us," "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you download and use the App. By using Space Dash, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account/Profile ID — a unique identifier generated for your profile so your progress can be saved and, if you choose, connected with friends.
  • Display Name — a name you choose to identify yourself to other players.
  • Profile Photo — an optional photo you may upload from your device.
  • Support Communications — if you contact us for help, we collect what you send us (such as your email address and a description of the issue).

1.2 Information Generated by Gameplay

  • Game statistics, including best distance, stars/currency collected, unlocked ships and trails, power-up inventory, and completed challenges.
  • Friend connections, friend requests, and leaderboard rankings, which may display your Display Name and score to other players.

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

  • Apple Game Center — if enabled, Game Center authenticates you and helps sync your saved progress across your devices. Data handled directly by Game Center is governed by Apple's own Privacy Policy.
  • Push Notifications — if you allow notifications, a device token is generated (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver notifications you've opted into, such as friend request alerts.
  • Advertising Identifiers — the App displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, general location (such as country/region), and other information to serve ads, including measuring ad performance and, where permitted by your device settings, personalized advertising.

1.4 Automatically Collected Information

Like most apps, we may automatically collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, general app usage patterns, and crash/diagnostic data, used to maintain stability and improve the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Save, sync, and restore your game progress across sessions and devices
  • Enable social features such as friend requests, friend lists, and leaderboards
  • Deliver notifications you have opted into
  • Display advertising to support free access to the App, and to offer optional in-app purchases
  • Diagnose bugs, maintain security, and improve App performance and features
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Game and account data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore) and, where applicable, Apple Game Center's own cloud storage. We retain your data for as long as your account/profile is active, or as needed to provide the App's features. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

4. Third-Party Services

Space Dash integrates the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which governs how it independently handles data:

Service Purpose Privacy Policy
Google Firebase (Firestore, Cloud Messaging) Save data, friends, leaderboards, push notifications https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
Google AdMob In-app advertising https://policies.google.com/privacy
Apple Game Center Optional cloud save & authentication https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If in-app purchases are enabled, all payment processing is handled directly by Apple's App Store; we do not receive or store your payment card details.

5. Children's Privacy

Space Dash is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details in Section 8 so we can promptly remove it.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Notifications: You can decline or revoke notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Profile Data: You can change your Display Name or remove your profile photo within the App at any time.
  • Ad Personalization: You can limit ad tracking through your device's privacy settings (e.g., Apple's "Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting).
  • Access/Deletion Requests: Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (for example, under the CCPA in California, GDPR in the EU/EEA, or similar state and national privacy laws). To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 8.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and where appropriate, we will provide additional notice in the App.
